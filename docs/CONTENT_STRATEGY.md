# Content Strategy

## Positioning

Working positioning:

```text
I help curious investors use AI-assisted research workflows to understand markets,
avoid obvious mistakes, and build better investment judgment.
```

This account should not be a signal-selling account. It should be a learning,
research, and workflow account.

## Audience Segments

- Busy retail investors who read market news but cannot synthesize it.
- Engineers/product people interested in investing but lacking market process.
- Investors curious about AI agents but unsure where to start.
- Trading/systematic investing learners who need guardrails against overfitting.
- Traditional investors who want better tools without becoming programmers.

## Content Pillars

### Market Understanding

- Weekly market environment.
- Breadth and regime checks.
- Theme and sector rotation.
- News impact analysis.
- Scenario planning.

### AI-Assisted Investing Workflow

- How to use agents for research.
- How to check data quality.
- How to avoid hallucinated analysis.
- How to turn news into scenarios.
- How to review a backtest.

### Tool and Demo Building

- Fast MVP demos for real investing pain points.
- Before/after workflow improvements.
- Playbooks people can use without coding.

### Learning and Postmortem

- Mistakes, wrong assumptions, and what changed.
- Strategy ideas rejected and why.
- Technical debt in research systems.
- Decision logs and investment process upgrades.

## Format Experiments

- Short X/Threads posts: one chart, one insight, one implication.
- Long threads: market regime or theme breakdown.
- Blog/newsletter: deeper weekly synthesis.
- Short video: "how I used an AI agent to analyze X".
- Demo launch post: pain point, workflow, link, feedback request.
- Carousel: framework or checklist.

## Default Post Structure

1. Hook: concrete pain, surprising observation, or useful question.
2. Context: what changed or why it matters.
3. Framework: how to think about it.
4. Evidence: chart, data, source, or example.
5. Caveat: what could invalidate the idea.
6. CTA: ask a question, invite feedback, link to demo, or save/share prompt.

## First Content Hypotheses

- People want market analysis that says "what matters this week" without pretending
  certainty.
- People are curious about AI agents but need tiny demos, not abstract explanations.
- Investment learners value checklists that prevent common mistakes.
- Transparent postmortems build more trust than only showing winning calls.
- Comparing "human workflow vs AI-assisted workflow" can attract both investors and
  builders.

## Cadence

First month:

- 3 short posts per week.
- 1 deeper analysis per week.
- 1 AI workflow/demo post per week.
- 1 weekly review note for internal learning.

Do not add more channels until there is a repeatable signal on one primary platform.
