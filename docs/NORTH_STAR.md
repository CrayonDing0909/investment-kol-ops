# North Star

The single direction anchor for this project. When a task, milestone, or piece of
content feels unclear, check it against this page first.

## We are building

A financial content product that turns markets into plain language using charts,
AI tools, and product design.

## One-line positioning

Not stock tips. Maps.

(不是報明牌，是做地圖。)

## Core audience

Tech workers, engineers, product people, designers, rational investing beginners,
and people who like charts and tools.

## Core promise

Every time you finish one of our artifacts, you understand one more thing: a
company, a supply chain, an ETF, or a market narrative.

## Content pillars

1. Supply Chain Maps
2. Company Cards
3. ETF X-ray
4. AI Research Tools
5. Cross-asset Market Maps

## We do not do

- No stock tips.
- No intraday buy/sell points.
- No paid stock recommendations.
- No "follow my trades" service.
- No treating second-hand podcast info as a primary source.
- No letting AI write the personal POV.

## First 90 days goal

Validate which artifact type best attracts high-quality audience to save, share,
comment, and subscribe.

## The operating loop

```text
Artifact -> Public content -> Audience signal -> Research deepening -> Product
```

Not:

```text
Research -> Brief -> Gate -> Maybe content
```

The first loop fits a creator and a product builder. The second fits an analyst.
We keep the analyst-grade research available, but it serves the creator loop.
