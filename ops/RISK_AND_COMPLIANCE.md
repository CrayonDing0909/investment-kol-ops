# Risk and Compliance

Purpose: keep the account trustworthy while publishing investment-related content.

## Default Disclaimer

```text
This is research and education, not financial advice. I may be wrong. Do your own
research and consider your own risk tolerance.
```

## Publishing Guardrails

- Do not promise returns.
- Do not imply certainty.
- Do not hide uncertainty or invalidation.
- Do not present backtests as future performance.
- Disclose positions or conflicts when relevant.
- Clearly label watchlists, scenarios, and hypotheses.
- Use sources for factual claims.
- Correct mistakes publicly when needed.

## High-Risk Content

Extra review required for:

- Specific buy/sell language.
- Leveraged products.
- Options.
- Crypto.
- Small-cap or low-liquidity names.
- Backtest results.
- Screenshots with private account data.
- Claims about AI tool performance.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- Is this educational or does it sound like personalized advice?
- Is the time horizon clear?
- Are risks and invalidation included?
- Are data sources identified?
- Are positions disclosed if relevant?
- Would this still be defensible if the market moves against it tomorrow?

## Data Privacy

- Do not publish brokerage account details.
- Remove API keys, account IDs, email addresses, and private notes from screenshots.
- Avoid uploading sensitive financial data to external demos.
