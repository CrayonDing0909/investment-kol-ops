三件 transcript 深讀後新發現

  1. CPU TAM 從 `$60B / +18% CAGR` 翻倍到 `>$120B / +35% CAGR`。Lisa Su 在 Q&A 明確 framing 為 doubling，不是普通 upgrade。同一管理層、同一公司、6 個月之內 TAM 雙倍上修是極罕見動作。讀法：這是 AMD 自家 management-expectation，後續看 IDC / Gartner / SemiAnalysis 第三方 TAM 是否同步上修。
  2. Q1 2026 server CPU 成長主要靠 unit volume，不是 ASP。Lisa Su Q&A：「actually much more unit driven...we are shipping more CPUs across, not just the high end Turing family, but we're actually shipping a lot of Genoa's, sort of the Zen 4 family as well.」修正 Mercury Q4 2025 ASP-driven 的 mental model：兩條同時成立——AMD 既有「premium installed base 高 ASP」（Q4 2025 截面），也在「邊際 unit volume 鋪量」（Q1 2026 邊際）。
  3. 10-Q 自承的 Counter：AMD 自己的 10-Q risk factors 明確點名 Nvidia-Intel 2025-09 partnership 為 material risk。這是做 AMD share-gain trajectory thesis 時最具體的 Counter。

引用 AMD 10-Q 原文（material risk）

"in September 2025, Nvidia announced a partnership and investment in Intel to partner on new data center and client platform products. This partnership may result in increased competition and pricing pressure for our products or could prevent us from participating in other opportunities, which could materially adversely impact our business, financial condition and margins."
