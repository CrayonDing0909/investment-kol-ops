## Why This Source Matters

Intel is the counterparty in the CPU revival thesis. If AMD is the winner side,
Intel is the defense case: Xeon demand, DCAI growth, advanced packaging, Foundry,
and the claim that CPUs are reasserting themselves as the AI orchestration layer.

## Raw Facts

- Q1 2026 revenue was $13.6B, up 7% YoY.
- Non-GAAP EPS was $0.29.
- Q2 2026 revenue guidance was $13.8B to $14.8B.
- Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the next wave of AI is moving from foundational models to inference to agentic, increasing the need for Intel CPUs and wafer / advanced packaging offerings.
- Intel CFO David Zinsner said Q1 results reflected the growing and essential role of the CPU in the AI era.
- Data Center and AI (DCAI) revenue was $5.1B, up 22% YoY.
- Intel Foundry revenue was $5.4B, up 16% YoY.
- Intel and Google announced a multiyear collaboration for continued deployment of Intel Xeon processors across Google workload-optimized instances.
- Intel and Google also co-developed custom ASIC infrastructure processing units (IPUs).
- Intel Xeon 6 was selected as the host CPU for NVIDIA DGX Rubin NVL8 systems.
- Intel and SambaNova announced a heterogeneous AI inference architecture combining SambaNova RDUs and Intel Xeon 6 processors.
- Transcript: Lip-Bu Tan said CPU now serves as the orchestration layer and control plane for the AI stack.
- Transcript: customers are deploying server CPUs alongside accelerators in a ratio moving back toward CPU.
- Transcript: DCAI saw strong ASIC growth, revenue up more than 30% sequentially and nearly doubled YoY.
- Transcript: Intel's server CPU demand outlook improved over the last 90 days, with strong industry unit growth extending into 2027.
- Transcript: memory, wafer, and substrate cost pressure could hurt demand later in the year.
