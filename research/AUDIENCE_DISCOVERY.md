# Audience Discovery

Purpose: understand people who do not use AI agents yet, then build simple MVP demos
that remove real friction.

## Target Users

- Investors overwhelmed by news and market data.
- People who want better research but do not know what to ask AI.
- People who use ChatGPT casually but not agent workflows.
- People who want portfolio feedback but fear complex tooling.
- People who have strategy ideas but cannot validate them properly.

## Pain Areas to Investigate

- "I read a lot but cannot decide what matters."
- "I do not know if this market analysis is reliable."
- "I do not know how to turn news into an investment thesis."
- "I want to use AI, but prompts feel too abstract."
- "I do not know if my backtest is fake or overfit."
- "I cannot maintain a watchlist or review process."
- "I want automation, but setup feels too technical."

## Interview Questions

1. When you research an investment idea, where do you start?
2. What part feels slow, confusing, or unreliable?
3. Have you tried using AI for this? What failed?
4. What would a useful 5-minute tool do for you?
5. What would make you trust or distrust the output?
6. What do you currently pay for or manually repeat?
7. What result would make you share the tool with a friend?

## MVP Selection Filter

Only build a demo if it passes:

- One painful workflow.
- One clear user.
- Result visible in under 5 minutes.
- Can be demoed in a post/video.
- Does not require sensitive brokerage credentials.
- Has a clear feedback question.
- Can be built with a simple UI first.

## Candidate MVPs

### Market Regime Explainer

User gives tickers or chooses a market. Tool returns regime, breadth, leadership,
risk zone, and scenario checklist.

### News to Scenario Analyzer

User pastes a headline. Tool returns first, second, and third-order effects plus
assets to watch.

### Backtest Sanity Checker

User uploads or pastes backtest summary. Tool checks lookahead bias, sample size,
fees, slippage, overfitting, and missing validation.

### Portfolio Risk Checklist

User enters holdings manually. Tool flags concentration, correlation, drawdown
exposure, missing thesis, and review dates.

### AI Investing Prompt Coach

Guided form that turns a vague question into a good agent prompt, then explains how
to verify the answer.

## Discovery Output

Each discovery cycle should produce:

- Pain statement.
- User quote.
- Existing workaround.
- MVP idea.
- Content angle.
- Validation metric.
