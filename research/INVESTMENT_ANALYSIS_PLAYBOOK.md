# Investment Analysis Playbook

Purpose: make market analysis repeatable, auditable, and suitable for public content.

## Analysis Stack

Use this order unless the task requires something narrower:

1. Macro regime.
2. Market breadth.
3. Top risk / distribution.
4. Sector and theme rotation.
5. News catalysts.
6. Technical structure.
7. Scenarios and invalidation.
8. Portfolio or position implications.

## Skill Mapping

When available, use the existing trading analysis skills:

- Market environment analysis for broad market context.
- Macro regime detector for structural regime change.
- Market breadth analyzer / uptrend analyzer for participation.
- Market top detector for tactical correction risk.
- Theme detector for sector and narrative rotation.
- Market news analyst for recent market-moving events.
- Technical analyst for chart-only scenario analysis.
- Scenario analyzer for 18-month second and third-order effects.
- Backtest expert for strategy validation and overfitting checks.
- Data quality checker before publishing market analysis.

## Quality Gates

- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Cite or name data sources.
- Avoid certainty language.
- State time horizon.
- State invalidation conditions.
- Check data units, dates, and ticker/instrument notation.
- Mention position/disclosure if relevant.

## Strategy and Backtest Quality Gates

- No lookahead bias.
- No survivorship bias when applicable.
- Include fees, spread, slippage, and execution assumptions.
- Show sample size and market regimes covered.
- Use out-of-sample or walk-forward validation when possible.
- Report drawdown, turnover, exposure, and failure conditions.
- Do not publish strategy claims without caveats.

## Content Boundary

The account can share:

- Frameworks.
- Watchlists.
- Scenarios.
- Research process.
- Tools and demos.
- Postmortems.

The account should avoid:

- Guaranteed returns.
- Urgent buy/sell calls.
- Hidden leverage or risk.
- Cherry-picked backtests.
- Advice personalized to a reader's financial situation.
